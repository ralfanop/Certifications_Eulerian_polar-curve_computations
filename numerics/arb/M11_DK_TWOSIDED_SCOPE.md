## Independent halves and additive phase bound

The first midpoint is enclosed by the corresponding exact initial-value ODE Taylor series, with bound 5*(h/R)^p/(1-h/R). At each midpoint recenter to an exact dyadic angle. Its normalized-arclength argument differs from the desired argument by a real phase shift: its magnitude is bounded by 4*angle_radius/(pi*ell). The autonomous ODE preserves that phase under every exact advancement; new recentering errors add to the existing phase, rather than multiplying angle balls by a sensitivity factor. Require the phase bound <h/2 so every midpoint and every advanced midpoint stays within the real chart. The phase-shifted curvature series use the same disk R. The degree-(p-1) angle advancement has tail <=5*(2h/R)^p/(1-2h/R).
