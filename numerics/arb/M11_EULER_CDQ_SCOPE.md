# M11 Eulerian length: recursive online convolution

Authoritative PDF SHA-256 `266cd62eddf952d4535a1964a7861b109f2a08d991e82d72dcd5f3c087a695b1` (30 pages). This is a scaling pilot following the merged PR #389, whose independent exact-head CI certified 60,039 relative bits at 85,000 working bits, in 1122.4 s, and 20,039 relative bits at 28,000 working bits. Neither result is a million-digit certificate.

The recurrence and rigorous uniform speed-square truncation remain unchanged. Within a block, given the two history convolutions C and D, solve the left half of the unknown block recursively. Multiply that half by the kernel a_j and the weighted kernel j*a_j; add only the resulting contributions to the right half. Then solve the right half recursively. At leaves use the exact coefficient recurrence directly. Each pair of indices i<k in a block contributes exactly once, either at their unique splitting node or within one leaf. Kernel chunks and retained K-term history are the same as in #389. This removes the quadratic direct inner-block loop as blocks grow, without discarding any term.

Convolution precision discounts are heuristics based on kernel decay; the discarding of bits is only a choice of working precision for enclosing Arb operations, not truncation of the enclosure. The full sum uses the original working precision. The same Cauchy tail and final independent relative-bit guard are applied; outputs retain exact dyadic endpoints and peak resident memory. The 100,000-bit CI stage is experimental and may be expensive. No previous certificate or manuscript is modified.
