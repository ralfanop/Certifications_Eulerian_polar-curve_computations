# M16 §9.5 — intrinsic restriction, spectral completeness and Hilbert complexification

Base: exact main 2ad7c37f3447a6d1cafd858f35832bb026feeccf (merge #690). Candidates only until CI confirms. Source scope: Proposition 9.6, Proposition 9.13, Corollary 9.14.

1. Intrinsic restriction: unit quaternion geodesic arcs x(cos t + j sin t) are actually on S³. Their derivatives at zero are xj. The chain rule now gives equality of directional derivatives of any two differentiable ambient extensions that agree on S³. Repeating the argument for curl and codifferential yields independence of the second-order Hodge coordinate operator. Thus the differential expressions descend to smooth restrictions, without assuming the extension is unique. Identifying that descended operator with the intrinsic Hodge star/exterior derivative on the entire smooth one-form space, and with the closed L² Hodge realization, remains to be formalized. The construction does not assert that all smooth sections admit a global ambient extension.

2. Eigenfamilies: Mathlib's compact self-adjoint spectral theorem (ContinuousLinearMap.orthogonalComplement_iSup_eigenspaces_eq_bot) gives total eigenspaces of a compact symmetric inverse. A nonzero inverse eigenvalue μ yields a graph eigenvector of the unbounded operator with eigenvalue μ⁻¹; zero cannot occur for a nonzero vector if the graph is single-valued. This discharges spectral totality at the abstract compact-inverse level. It does not yet construct compact inverses of the concrete differential Hodge and Dirac operators, select and enumerate their individual geometric eigenvectors with the specified multiplicities, or certify their smoothness. Theorem #673 still supplies the Hilbert basis from a complete orthonormal family. Classical references Bär and Ikeda–Taniguchi remain explicitly identified as external inputs.

3. Hilbert complexification: adapt Mario Ullrich's Lean-SNumbers BasicResults/Spectral/Complexification.lean (Apache 2.0, original copyright retained; upstream blob SHA in source header; license in third_party). The adapted module isolates namespace M16ComplexHilbert, uses the pinned Mathlib imports, and adds explicit axiom reports. It constructs the genuine complex scalar and inner-product structures, their norm, completeness, real isometric embedding, complexification of bounded maps, exact preservation of operator norm and adjoints. M16IntrinsicBridge connects the actual complex Hilbert type to the real/imaginary graph construction from #673, deriving closedness and full adjoint graph equality on that type. The geometric spaces and their unbounded operators still require actual instantiation; dense graph domain in this norm and compactness of complexified bounded maps remain follow-up obligations. Imported code is not represented as original to M16 or independently proven here.
