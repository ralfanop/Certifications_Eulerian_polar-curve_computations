## Analytic proposition (verified by Fourier multiplication)

Fix `κ ∈ ℝ²`, `k>0`, and `|κ|>k`. Let `Hκ = -d²/dz²+|κ|²` with domain `H²(ℝ)`, and `α²=|κ|²-k²>0`. Then the bounded inverse `Rκ(k²)=(Hκ-k²)⁻¹` exists and

`‖Rκ(k²)‖_{L²→L²}=1/α²`.

Indeed the unitary Fourier transform identifies `Hκ-k²` with multiplication by `ξ²+α²`; its inverse is multiplication by `1/(ξ²+α²)`, whose essential supremum is `1/α²`. Equality follows from testing functions supported in arbitrarily small intervals about `ξ=0`. The inverse is self-adjoint and positive; therefore for all `f∈L²(ℝ)`, `Im ⟨f,Rκ(k²)f⟩=0`. The kernel `e^{-α|z-z'|}/(2α)` is consistent with this multiplier, but the kernel identity requires a separate Fourier-transform or distributional Green-function proof.

For `0<k₁,k₂<|κ|`, the resolvent identity is
`Rκ(k₁²)-Rκ(k₂²)=(k₁²-k₂²)Rκ(k₁²)Rκ(k₂²)`.
This follows pointwise from the scalar multipliers; no trace-class assertion is implied.
