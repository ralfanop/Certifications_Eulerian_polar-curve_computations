\par\medskip\noindent\textit{A classical inverse-central-binomial expansion.}
Conway and Guy~\cite{conwayguy1996}, p.~242, discuss the Gregory angles
$t_n=\arctan(1/n)$, including $t_2=\arctan(1/2)$; that page does not
contain the inverse-binomial series below.
Set $C_k^{(B)}=\operatorname{Cat}(B_k)=\binom{2k}{k}$,
the type-$B$ Catalan number, as in Petersen~\cite{petersen2015},
Sect.~12.3, p.~277; see also OEIS A000984~\cite{oeis000984}.
Sprugnoli~\cite{sprugnoli2006}, Theorem~2.4 (2006), established
\[
 \sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{(4u)^k}{(2k+1)C_k^{(B)}}=
 \frac1u\sqrt{\frac{u}{1-u}}\arctan\sqrt{\frac{u}{1-u}},
 \qquad 0<u<1.
\]
At $u=1/5$ this yields
\[
 \arctan\frac12=\frac25\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}
 \frac{(4/5)^k}{(2k+1)C_k^{(B)}},
\]
also recorded in OEIS A073000~\cite{oeis073000}.
Indeed the beta integral gives
\[
 \frac1{(2k+1)\binom{2k}{k}}=
 \int_0^1 s^k(1-s)^k\,\mathrm ds,
\]
and termwise summation of the nonnegative geometric series yields
\[
 \frac25\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{(4/5)^k}{(2k+1)\binom{2k}{k}}
 =\frac25\int_0^1\frac{\mathrm ds}{1-\tfrac45s(1-s)}
 =\int_{-1}^1\frac{\mathrm dv}{4+v^2}=\arctan\frac12.
\]
The already established histogram angle and its normalisation satisfy
\[
 \alpha_3=\frac45\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}
 \frac{(4/5)^k}{(2k+1)C_k^{(B)}},\qquad
 p_\gamma=\frac{2}{5\pi}\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}
 \frac{(4/5)^k}{(2k+1)C_k^{(B)}}.
\]
These are classical representations of known constants, not new
theorems of Manuscript~15 nor evidence for a structural equivalence
between Eulerian and type-$B$ Catalan families.
