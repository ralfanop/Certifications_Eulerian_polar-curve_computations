## What the revision adds

The revision adds Subsection 5.8, *The rate of convergence in the proportional regime.*, with four results.

- **Lemma 5.11 (Freud's identity).** `k/a_k = M ∫ φ_M' p_k p_{k-1} g_M dx`, for `M ≥ 3` and `k ≥ 1`.
- **Lemma 5.12.** Calculus for smooth compactly supported functions of Jacobi operators whose off-diagonal entries lie in [0, 1/2]:
  - off-diagonal decay `(1+|k-i|+|i-l|)^{-5}` of the differences, proved by Fourier inversion and Duhamel's formula;
  - a symmetric kernel `B_{ki}` that is positive semidefinite when the function is nondecreasing on [-1, 1].
- **Lemma 5.13 (localisation).**
  - The tail mass of `p_k^2 g_M` outside `|x| < r_K` is at most `e^{-c_K M}`.
  - The tail integral of `|φ_M' p_k p_{k-1}| g_M` is at most `2(k+1)^2 e^{-c_K M}`.
- **Theorem 5.14.** For every compact `K ⊂ (0,∞)` there are `C_K` and `M_K` such that, for `M ≥ M_K` and `j/M ∈ K`:
  - `|β_j^[M]/M^2 − F(j/M)| ≤ C_K/M`;
  - `|(β_j^[M])^{1/2}/M − ξ_{j/M}/2| ≤ C_K/M`.

The proof compares the true coefficients, which satisfy Freud's identity, with the model sequence `ξ_{k/M}/2` on a window of half-width `t_1 M/4`. For the constant sequence, Freud's identity reduces to the Mhaskar–Rakhmanov–Saff equation `Φ(ξ_t) = t`. A weighted strong monotonicity estimate, which uses the convexity of the Cramér rate function `I`, then gives the rate.
