# Manuscript 14: Lean 4 and Arb certification record

*Magnetic properties of the Eulerian profile at the special point* $i/\sqrt2$, version 14.118. Roberto Alfano, 8 November 2026. A PDF version of this record is [Manuscript14_Certification.pdf](Manuscript14_Certification.pdf).

## 1. Scope.

This record states, for each numbered result of manuscript 14, version 14.118, and for the computed values added to its text on 8 November 2026, what is kernel-checked in Lean 4, which scalar values are enclosed by Arb at 2000 bits, which steps are classical inputs and which formalisation tasks remain open. Equation, section and result numbers are those of version 14.118, identical to those of version 14.105: up to (141) they are those of version 14.91, and from (142) on they exceed those of version 14.91 by one, because version 14.95 added the line (142) with fifty decimals of the root $x_0$ of (139). All certificates are in the main branch of the repository <https://github.com/ralfanop/Certifications_Eulerian_polar-curve_computations> at commit [`4909a891`](https://github.com/ralfanop/Certifications_Eulerian_polar-curve_computations/tree/4909a8910c74f4a7c31c2145b9466fbedd5a781e), and were audited from 5 to 8 November 2026 (those of versions 14.95–14.105 in [PR 63](https://github.com/ralfanop/Certifications_Eulerian_polar-curve_computations/pull/63), those of version 14.118 in [PR 64](https://github.com/ralfanop/Certifications_Eulerian_polar-curve_computations/pull/64)); each pull request (PR) below links its workflow runs. The Lean certificates use Lean `v4.35.0-rc3` with the pinned Mathlib dependency recorded in their workflows, compiled from source; every axiom audit lists only `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`, with no `sorryAx` and no `Lean.ofReduceBool`. The numerical certificates use `python-flint 0.9.0`, exact rational arithmetic and Arb balls at 2000 bits; they certify the stated scalar quantities and do not prove infinite-dimensional operator identities.
