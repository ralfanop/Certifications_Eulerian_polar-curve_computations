# Manuscript 14: Lean 4 and Arb certification record

*Eulerian Fourier profiles at the special point* $\mathrm i/\sqrt2$, version 14.91. Roberto Alfano, 7 November 2026. A PDF version of this record is [Manuscript14_Certification.pdf](Manuscript14_Certification.pdf).

## 1. Scope.

This record states, for each numbered result of manuscript 14, version 14.91, what is kernel-checked in Lean 4, which scalar values are enclosed by Arb at 2000 bits, which steps are classical inputs and which formalisation tasks remain open. Equation, section and result numbers are those of version 14.91. All certificates are in the main branch of the repository <https://github.com/ralfanop/Certifications_Eulerian_polar-curve_computations> at commit [`38957482`](https://github.com/ralfanop/Certifications_Eulerian_polar-curve_computations/tree/38957482b9d70d2660b36eaeaa6652490fc39343), and were audited from 5 to 7 November 2026; each pull request (PR) below links its workflow runs. The Lean certificates use Lean `v4.35.0-rc3` with the pinned Mathlib dependency recorded in their workflows, compiled from source; every axiom audit lists only `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`, with no `sorryAx` and no `Lean.ofReduceBool`. The numerical certificates use `python-flint 0.9.0`, exact rational arithmetic and Arb balls at 2000 bits; they certify the stated scalar quantities and do not prove infinite-dimensional operator identities.

## 4. Classical inputs.

The following enter the certificates as explicit hypotheses or are not reproved: the inputs of Section 2.2 of the manuscript, among them the surface measure $\mathrm d u\,\mathrm d\varphi/(4\pi)$ of $S^2$ in axial coordinates; the identification of the Fredholm determinant and trace of a positive trace-class operator with the product and sum over its spectrum; the quasi-power approximation $A_n(u)=B(u)^{n+1}[1+O(2^{-n})]$ (Flajolet–Sedgewick, Example IX.37); the real, simple, negative zeros of $A_n$ (Frobenius); the determinantal structure of the descent process (Borodin–Diaconis–Fulman, Theorem 5.1); the $j$-values in (21); the covering-space statements of Theorem 8.1(i) and the exact sequence (112); and the definition of the spheroidal functions in DLMF §§30.2–30.3.

## 5. Open formalisation tasks.

Not formalised in Lean: the identification of the radial operators with $\mathsf H_2$, $\mathsf H_3$ at the origin (Proposition 3.1); the uniqueness of the regular radial solution (Proposition 3.3); Proposition 4.1 on the sphere itself; the zeta-regularised determinants (Proposition 6.2); Proposition 6.5; the unitary splitting $UA_2U^{-1}=A\oplus A_{\mathrm{DN}}$ (Theorem 7.1); the boundary value (122) (Proposition 8.4); part (ii) of Proposition 8.6 for general $m$, and (128); the operator statements of Proposition 8.7; the extension of $\omega_{\mathrm{Bl}}$ to $\zeta=\infty$ and the homeomorphism of Theorem 9.3; the invariance of $\mu$ itself under $\Theta$ (Corollary 9.4). For Proposition 2.1 and Corollary 2.2 this record lists only the numerical certificates. The data of the figures of Section 4, computed from the exact Eulerian numbers up to $n=670$, carry no certificate.
