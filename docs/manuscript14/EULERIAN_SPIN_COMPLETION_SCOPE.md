# Global finite spin inverse, quantitative logarithmic remainder, classical conjugate and Fisher saturation distance

Stacked on PR #728. No physical-field generation is asserted. The source
uses the actual finite Gibbs law and existing Langevin range/support.

Targets: global strict monotonicity and range (-1,1), two-sided inverse
on that interval, quantitative remainder n^2(n+1)J(1-q) for q>=0,
classical conjugate lower bound log(2/(1-m))-1, divergence as m approaches
1 from below and unbounded endpoint objective (not real sSup at endpoint).
For positive t, Fisher variance <= n^3 exp(-t), hence length to the
positive saturation boundary <= 2 sqrt(n^3) exp(-t/2), tending to zero.
Distance is defined as supremum of oriented arc lengths in the
one-dimensional spin family, not a claim about ambient Fisher-Rao
distances or a completed general Riemannian manifold.
