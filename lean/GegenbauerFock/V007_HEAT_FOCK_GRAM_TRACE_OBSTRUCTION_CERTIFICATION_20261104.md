## 1. Actual operators, not proxy diagonal models

Let `lambda>0`, `alpha>0`, `s>0`.

- `H_s` is the genuine positive-time heat semigroup on the real weighted space `L²(nu_lambda;R)`, generated by the canonical closed polynomial Gegenbauer Sturm differential operator.
- `G_(lambda,alpha) = C_(lambda,alpha)^* C_(lambda,alpha)` is the genuine positive complex Gegenbauer–Fock Gram on `L²(nu_lambda;C)`.

The previously kernel-checked real-S1 and complex-CFC trace theorems give the **two distinct** identities

```text
realNuclearInfimum(H_s)
   = SUM_{n>=0} exp(-s*n*(n+2*lambda)),

ProbabilisticTheory.traceNorm(G_(lambda,alpha))
   = KummerCoefficients.kummerSeries(lambda, 1/(4*alpha)).
```

Both operators and their trace norms are actually constructed in V007. Neither identity is an assumption about an unknown spectral model.

## 2. General trace-gap obstruction — certified

The theorem

`no_heat_fockGram_basis_intertwiner_of_trace_gap`



## 5. Logical and editorial limits

- The no-go theorem excludes **this specified type of complete-eigenbasis-preserving real-linear intertwiner**. It does not exclude every conceivable non-injective, non-basis-preserving, non-spectral or partial relation between the operators.
- The strict numerical obstruction is proved at `(lambda,alpha,s)=(1/2,1,1/10)`. It is **not** a statement of inequivalence for all time and all parameters. The general theorem applies at any positive parameters only when a trace gap is separately known.
- The real and complex trace norms are different *definitions* (optimal real projective rank-one infimum and source-compatible complex CFC absolute trace norm). Their trace identities are independently kernel certified. The no-go proof does not assume a general categorical equivalence of all real and complex Schatten APIs.
- This is a certified **obstruction to a proposed identification**, not a proof of a new spectral description of the Gram and not a bibliographical novelty claim.
- Do not merge PR #16 or transfer into V097. V007 remains independent.
