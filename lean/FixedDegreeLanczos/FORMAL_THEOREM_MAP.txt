V033 - Formal theorem map (21 October 2026)

Notation: M is the positive integer number of uniform summands.
The final Lean theorems index the positive degree as j+1, where j : Nat.
Substituting manuscript degree n=j+1 gives:
  beta_n(M) = n*M/12 - n*(n-1)/20 + O_n(1/M).

FINAL STATEMENTS
1. uniform_integral_hankel_two_term concerns literal integrals on a probability
   space of sums of a measurable independent uniform family. There is no
   assumed orthogonal polynomial family in this theorem. The relevant
   determinant is proved eventually nonzero, and a C/M error bound is proved.
2. uniform_probability_two_term concerns the manuscript's monic family.
   Its additional hypotheses spell out degree, monicity, integral orthogonality,
   P_0=1, P_1=X, and the defining three-term recurrence. The norm/determinant
   formula for beta is proved, not supplied as a hypothesis.
3. canonical_two_term has no probability or polynomial-family parameters:
   it proves the bound and eventual nonvanishing for the exactly defined
   uniform-sum moment recurrence. The probability bridge identifies that
   recurrence with actual uniform-sum integrals.

Finite algebra route: actual moments -> exact cumulant polynomials -> Gaussian
constant term and fourth-Hermite correction -> diagonal Gaussian Gram matrix
-> first determinant coefficient -> exact quadratic polynomial remainder
-> rational-function continuity and eventual nonzero denominator -> C/M bound.
An Edgeworth theorem and uniform density expansions are not used.

ASSUMPTIONS / LIMITS
- The distribution is the ordinary uniform law on [-1/2,1/2]; the Lean law uses
  the half-open interval Ioc. Endpoint equivalence with the closed interval
  is proved in V033UniformSumMoments.lean.
- Every fixed positive degree is covered. The constants and threshold are
  existential and degree-dependent. They are not optimized or explicitly
  numerically evaluated. No joint growing-degree asymptotic is claimed.
- Gaussian polynomial expectations are defined by the exact Gaussian moment
  recurrence and proved positive. Identifying them with a separately defined
  Gaussian measure is unnecessary for this algebraic proof and is not claimed.
- An infinite orthogonal family is not separately constructed. Its defining
  properties are the explicit hypotheses of the recurrence-family theorem.
  The canonical and integral-Hankel results do not rely on that hypothesis.
- All-positive-M pole exclusion for every original expanded JSON denominator
  remains a paper argument. Eventual nonvanishing for the general asymptotic
  theorem is now proved in Lean.
- Originality/priority is not certified. The finite inputs are unchanged.
